SAMI

Independent video game currently in development.

SAMI is an independent pixel art psychological horror game focused on exploration, puzzle-solving, and survival. Explore the house, solve its mysteries, and collect items that will help you uncover the truth. Hide from the monster, survive, and discover what lies beneath the house.

Protocolo: Nueve Vidas

Independent post-apocalyptic action game currently in development.

You play as a cyborg on a mission to rescue your kidnapped cat, Nueve Vidas, across a devastated and futuristic Gran Concepción. Explore the city, rescue other cats, help survivors, destroy infected nests, and fight hostile robots standing in your way.
